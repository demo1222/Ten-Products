## Risk Management & Contingencies

### Key Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Scope creep | High | High | Strict change management, prioritization |
| Integration delays | Medium | High | Early integration testing, clear APIs |
| Performance issues | Medium | Medium | Load testing, optimization buffer |
| Security vulnerabilities | Low | Critical | Security reviews, vulnerability scanning |
| Team unavailability | Low | High | Cross-training, documentation |

### Contingency Plans
- **Buffer Time**: 1 week added for unknown issues
- **Phased Approach**: MVP → Phase 2 features if needed
- **Rollback Plan**: Ready to revert to previous stable version
- **Support Plan**: Dedicated support team on standby
